## Family Health

**Tags:** jss1, basic-science, theme-1, family-health

## Environmental Pollution
**Theme:** Learning About Our Environment  
**Citation:** NERDC JSS1 Basic Science · Theme 1 (Learning About Our Environment) · Topic: Environmental Pollution
**Tags:** jss1, basic-science, theme-1, environmental-pollution

**Learning objectives (a learner should be able to):**
- Define air, soil and water pollution
- Identify various pollutants and their effects
- List various causes of pollution
- Mention ways of reducing the risk of pollution
- Describe pollution control

**Content:**
- Definition of air, soil and water pollution
- Causes of air, soil and water pollution e.g. domestic/industrial waste, fertilizer/insecticide, agricultural, chemical, non-biodegradable, oil spillage
- Consequences of pollution such as respiratory tract infection, water-borne diseases, destruction of soil organisms and poor plant yield
- Control measures

**Understanding checks (sample question seeds):**
- Define pollution
- Name types of pollution and give specific examples
- state 3 major causes of air, soil and water pollution in the community
- Briefly explain the consequences of pollution to the environment and man
- Write a report on the field visit to polluted sites
- State effects of pollutants
- Suggest at least two ways on how to control pollution

## Living and Non - Living Things
**Theme:** Learning About Our Environment  
**Citation:** NERDC JSS1 Basic Science · Theme 1 (Learning About Our Environment) · Topic: Living and Non - Living Things
**Tags:** jss1, basic-science, theme-1, living-and-non---living-things

**Learning objectives (a learner should be able to):**
- Recognize that all living and non-living things are made up of matter
- list the three states of matter
- Collect and identify samples of living and non-living things in their environment
- list the distinguishing characteristics of living and non-living things
- State the importance of plants and animals to human beings
- collect samples of some living and non-living things, sort out the materials and explain the uses

**Content:**
- Meaning, identification and classification of matter
- States of matter
- Characteristics of living and non-living things
- Examples, Characteristics and importance of plants and animals
- Differences between plants and animals
- Examples, properties, uses and importance of living and non-living things
- Classification of non-living things into metals and non-metals

**Understanding checks (sample question seeds):**
- Define matter
- list 3 states of matter and classify objects according to their state of matter
- name four examples of plants and animals
- state four differences between plants and animals
- List three important uses of plants and animals
- list four characteristics of living and non-living things
- Describe self as a living thing
- list five activities of living thing and state the organs associated with each
- list five examples each of metals and non-metals

## Renewable and Non-Renewable Energy
**Theme:** You and Energy

**Tags:** jss1, basic-science, theme-2, renewable-and-non-renewable-energy

## Forces
**Theme:** You and Energy  
**Citation:** NERDC JSS1 Basic Science · Theme 2 (You and Energy) · Topic: Forces
**Tags:** jss1, basic-science, theme-2, forces

**Learning objectives (a learner should be able to):**
- Explain the meaning of force
- Identify contact and non-contact forces
- Differentiate between magnetic and gravitational forces
- measure and calculate gravitational force (GF) when mass and height are provided
- Describe how to set up a balanced force
- Explain the meaning of friction, its uses, advantages and disadvantages

**Content:**
- Meaning and types of force: contact and non-contact forces, magnetic and gravitational forces
- Calculation of gravitational force
- Balanced and unbalanced forces
- Friction: uses, advantages and disadvantages

**Understanding checks (sample question seeds):**
- Explain the meaning of force
- Name two contact and two non-contact forces
- State the differences between magnetic and gravitational forces
- Calculate gravitational force of an object when mass and height are given
- Describe how to set up a balanced force
- Explain the meaning of friction and state two uses and disadvantages of friction

## Science and development
**Theme:** Science and Development  
**Citation:** NERDC JSS1 Basic Science · Theme 3 (Science and Development) · Topic: Science and development
**Tags:** jss1, basic-science, theme-3, science-and-development

**Learning objectives (a learner should be able to):**
- Explain gravitation, weightlessness, satellite, and space travel
- State the effects of gravitation
- Identify the components of the solar system and explain the rotation and revolution of the earth and the moon
- Illustrate the eclipse of the sun and the moon
- Explain the seasons of the year

**Content:**
- Gravitation and weightlessness
- The earth in space
- Space travel
- Satellite

**Understanding checks (sample question seeds):**
- Define gravitational pull
- Describe the effect of gravitation on stone, feather and list components of the solar system
- Explain what causes: night and day, seasons of the year
- Explain what causes of eclipse of the sun and the moon
- Explain space travel
- State two purposes of space travel
- Mention 2 benefits and 2 dangers of space travel
- Mention 3 uses of satellite
